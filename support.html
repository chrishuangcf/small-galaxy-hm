Support

Need a hand with small galaxy.? Start with the answers below. If you are still stuck, we are happy to help.

Contact us

Email photodesignch@gmail.com and include your device (for example iPhone 15 or iPad Air), your iOS version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot helps too. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Pick a civilization and a charter, then give orders each round: navigate, develop, research, trade, and sign pacts. Drag to pan, switch to Rotate to orbit the chart, and pinch to zoom.

Is there multiplayer?

No. It is a single-player game, and the AI rivals run on your device.

Does it need the internet?

No. The game, including its music, runs entirely on your device and works offline.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, resets it, and we are not able to recover it.

The game feels slow or looks wrong.

Close other apps, restart the game, and make sure your device is running the latest iOS. If it continues, email us with your device model.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad.
